Privacy Policy — Zombie Tower Defense

Last updated: July 15, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Zombie Tower Defense ("the app", "we") handles information when you play the game on Android or on the web. The app is developed and published by Mohamed Ali Torjmen ("the developer"). Contact: torjmenmedali@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

The app does not ask for your name, address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The following data is processed automatically:

  • Game progress (cloud save). Your in-game progress (levels, coins, gems, upgrades, scores) is saved to Google Firebase Firestore under an anonymous, randomly generated account ID so it can be restored if you reinstall or switch devices. It is also saved to Google Play Games Saved Games if you are signed in to Google Play Games.
  • Leaderboard entries. If you choose a nickname, that nickname and your scores are stored and shown to other players on the in-game leaderboards (weekly, monthly and all-time).
  • Analytics. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played (e.g. app opens, screens visited, battles played, purchases and ad views). This includes an app-instance identifier and general device information (model, OS version, country, language).
  • Advertising identifiers. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID and use device information to serve and measure ads. Depending on your device settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.
  • Play Games profile. If you sign in to Google Play Games, your public Play Games profile (gamer tag and avatar) is used for achievements and Google leaderboards, per Google's own policies.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details.

2. How we use this information

  • To save and restore your game progress across devices.
  • To operate leaderboards, contests, and achievements.
  • To show ads that fund the free game, and to verify rewarded-ad completions.
  • To understand aggregate gameplay and improve balance, stability, and features.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for any purpose other than running the game.

3. Third-party services

The app uses the following services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Games — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Firestore, Remote Config) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob — AdMob privacy information
  • Google Play Billing — policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data retention & deletion

Cloud saves and leaderboard entries are kept while your anonymous account exists. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. To request deletion of your cloud save, leaderboard entries, or any other data associated with your game, email torjmenmedali@gmail.com from within the app's support channel and we will delete it within 30 days. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device's Google settings.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It contains cartoon violence and is intended for a general/teen audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on Google's infrastructure. Access to player data is restricted by Firebase security rules so that each player can only write their own save.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
