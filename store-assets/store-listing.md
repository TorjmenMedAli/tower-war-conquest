## Full description (4000 chars max)

🧟 **THE HORDE IS COMING. YOUR CANNON IS READY.**

Zombie Tower Defense is a fast, colorful tower-defense battle: mount weapons on your rolling war wagon, deploy special forces, unleash hero ultimates — and mow down waves of the undead across a ruined world.

⚔️ **FIGHT THROUGH THE APOCALYPSE**
• 10-level campaign across zombie-infested cities, graveyards, sewers, swamps, carnivals and more
• Epic boss fights at the end of every stage
• Endless Survival mode — how long can you last?

🔫 **BUILD YOUR ARSENAL**
• Unlock and upgrade a full arsenal of weapons, from rifles to rocket launchers
• Mount up to 2 weapons on your 3-stage war wagon and upgrade it into a rolling fortress
• Grow your castle stronghold for extra survivability

🦸 **COLLECT HEROES & FORCES**
• Recruit heroes of every rarity, each with a unique ultimate ability
• Level them up, master them, and earn new heroes by clearing levels
• Deploy Special Forces squads mid-battle to turn the tide

🏆 **COMPETE EVERY WEEK**
• Weekly AND monthly contests — top 10 players win gem prizes!
• Global all-time leaderboard
• Google Play Games achievements and leaderboards
• Cloud save: your progress follows you to any device

🎁 **ALWAYS SOMETHING TO COME BACK FOR**
• Daily missions and a 7-day login streak calendar
• Daily free chests and reward boxes
• Your tower keeps earning coins while you're away!

Can you survive the horde? Download free and start blasting! 🧟💥
